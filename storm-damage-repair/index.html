How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Take dated photos of the house every year, since insurers ask about prior condition
  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams
  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation
  • Trim trees near your home to lower the risk of wind-driven branch damage
  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often
  • If storm damage breaks a pipe you will need the main shutoff, so find it and practice now

Warning Signs for Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Gutters or downspouts torn loose, bent or dumping water against the house
  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up
  • Attic or wall insulation that is damp or matted down following a storm
  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor
  • From ground level, the roof shows shingles torn off, broken or raised
  • Swollen ceiling panels or blistered, peeling paint in the days after a storm
  • Wind-driven rain coming in through windows, doors or wall penetrations
