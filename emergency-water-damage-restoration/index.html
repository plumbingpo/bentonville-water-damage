Signs It Is Time for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Part of your ceiling bows downward because water is pooling on top of it.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.
  • Sewage has backed up, or the water is dark with a foul odor.
  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.
  • Several hours or more have passed with hardwood, drywall or carpet sitting wet.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.

How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Wrap plumbing in cold, unheated parts of the house so a hard freeze cannot crack it.
  • Any trip beyond a few days is a reason to turn the main supply off before you go.
  • Place leak sensors near the water heater, washing machine, dishwasher and under sinks.
  • Every member of the household should know where the main water valve is and how it closes.
  • Replace old supply hoses on washers, toilets and faucets before they burst.
  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
